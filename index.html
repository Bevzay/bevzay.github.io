E-commerce Specialist

My core focus is online stores — order processing, inventory coordination, product listings, and the day-to-day operations that keep a shop running smoothly. That said, I'm just as comfortable supporting service-based businesses that need the same level of admin structure.

Platform-Agnostic

Whether your store or site runs on Shopify, Trade Me, WooCommerce, or something custom-built, I can jump in and get productive fast. No lengthy onboarding — I adapt to your existing tools instead of asking you to change them.

Strategic AI Use

I use AI as a tool to speed up copywriting and first drafts, not as a replacement for judgment. That keeps my time focused on the operations, coordination, and decision-making that actually move your business forward.

Proactive Problem-Solver

I don't wait for things to break. Part of my role is watching for bottlenecks — a slow process, a gap in communication, a task quietly piling up — and flagging or fixing them before they become bigger issues.

Consistent Monthly Availability

This isn't a one-off project or a freelancer you'll have to re-brief every few months. I work with clients on an ongoing monthly basis, so I stay familiar with your business and can be relied on month after month.
